## Full description

Feed the hungriest hole in the universe — but only with the right color!
Drag your chroma hole around the arena and swallow blobs, crystals and squishy
shapes that match your color. Every snack makes you bigger; every wrong bite
shrinks you down. Dive through paint pools to instantly switch colors and keep
the feast going.

Grow big enough, wear the right color, and devour the giant goal capsule to
clear the level before the clock runs out. Forty handcrafted-feeling levels
ramp up from a relaxing two-color snack bar to a five-color frenzy with moving
targets and drifting paint pools — plus spectacular mega capsules every ten
levels. Earn stars for speed and precision, collect coins, and unlock a dozen
gorgeous hole skins with glows, patterns and particle trails.

One finger. No rules to read. Impossible to put down. How big can your hole
get?
