CHROMA HOLE

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Chroma Hole ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you play the game. Chroma Hole is a single-player arcade game with no user accounts, no chat, and no way to contact other players.

1. Information We Collect Directly

Chroma Hole itself does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on remote servers. All of your gameplay data - coins, unlocked skins, level progress, and star ratings - is stored only in your device's local app storage and never leaves your device or is sent to us. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes this data.

2. Advertising

Chroma Hole shows ads (interstitial and rewarded video ads) through Google AdMob to keep the game free to play. To serve and measure ads, Google AdMob and its advertising partners may automatically collect certain technical information from your device, such as your advertising identifier, IP address, device type, and general ad interaction data (for example, which ad was shown or watched). We do not receive or store this information ourselves - it is collected and processed directly by Google in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy and AdMob's data collection practices.

You can review or reset your advertising identifier, and control ads-personalization, in your device's system settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads).

3. In-App Purchases

Chroma Hole offers one optional cosmetic in-app purchase (the "Prism" hole skin). Purchases are processed entirely by the Google Play billing system; we do not receive or store your payment details.

4. Children's Privacy

Chroma Hole is suitable for a general audience and contains no objectionable content. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Advertising shown in the app is served by Google AdMob as described above; if the app is listed under Google Play's Families program, ads are served in accordance with Google's applicable child-directed treatment settings for that listing.

5. Your Rights

Because we do not collect or store any personal data on our own servers, there is no personal data held by us to access, correct, or delete. For any data collected by Google AdMob, you can exercise your rights (access, deletion, opting out of personalized ads, etc.) directly through your Google Account privacy settings.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
