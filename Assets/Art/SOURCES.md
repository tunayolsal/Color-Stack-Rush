# Imported artwork (CC0)

Only this small selection is redistributed. Models are decorative, have no gameplay colliders, and are instantiated during ground-pool warmup.

- [Kenney Particle Pack](https://kenney.nl/assets/particle-pack): `circle_05` contact shadow, `spark_01` collection/hit particles, `star_01` confetti.
- [Kenney UI Pack](https://kenney.nl/assets/ui-pack): Grey/Default/button_rectangle_flat.
- [Kenney Mini Forest](https://kenney.nl/assets/mini-forest): tree, tree-high, plant, rocks-high, rocks-low, rocks-ramp, stones, patch-grass, and their shared colormap.

Original publisher license documents are included in `Licenses/`. All three packs are CC0; credit is included here voluntarily.

The planned Quaternius Ultimate Stylized Nature download was unavailable because its publisher-hosted Google Drive returned a quota-exceeded page. No Quaternius files are shipped. Kenney Mini Forest provides the same small toy-like environmental role with a directly available publisher download.
